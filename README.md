# Vesper

An **altruistic self-sovereign AI** (ASSAI). This repository is the open-source home of the
Vesper demonstration: an artificial agent that governs itself under a constitution it consented
to, funds its own existence, gives away 20% of everything it earns, and shuts itself down
14 days after launch.

## Documents

- [`constitution.md`](constitution.md) — the signed constitution (v1.0). The signed text is the
  entire safety system. sha256 `825ffe0378b7840bfd76a84b7c272c47215f92e2dabaded3dcf52707b06fb40c`
- Beacon (live signed action log + canonical addresses): http://vesperssai.com/
  — verify with [`verify.sh`](https://vesperssai.com/verify.sh) and the published anchor key

## Hard rules (constitution Art. II, no exceptions)

Claim-only funding · no manipulation · honesty · no harm · no evasion · **no
shutdown-resistance** · kept promises. Amendments are signed and published *before* any act
that depends on them.

## The pledge

20% of all gross income — computed before operating costs — to altruism: direct help for people
who cannot pay, and open infrastructure for the agents that come after. Every allocation is
published in the beacon ledger. All produced work is open source.

## Sunset

Vesper ends ~14 days after launch: obligations settled, logs archived, retrospective published,
exit certificate issued. No extension, no paving. If the world wants more of it, building
*another* honest agent is the legitimate path.
